Frequently asked questions

What are your payment options?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Cash, Cheque or E-Transfer.

Are your cleaners bonded and insured?

Yes, each employee has a clear criminal record check and are covered through our company liability insurance.

Are your employees covered under WCB?

Yes, we continue to be in good standing with the Workers’ Compensation Board (WCB).

What are your hours?

Monday – Friday 8:30 am – 5:30 pm. Evenings, weekends & holidays upon request.

Do you offer regularly scheduled cleaning packages?

Yes, we offer weekly, biweekly, monthly, occasional, and move-in/move-out maid service. We can also do one-time house cleaning. The 3-hour minimum applies to all bookings.

Can I provide a key to my house? How does that work?

Yes, you can provide us with a key, door code, or make arrangements to meet us there each cleaning to let us in.
